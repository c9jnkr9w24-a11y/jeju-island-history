Culture & landscape

A living relationship with the sea.

Jeju is not defined by history alone. Its landscape and maritime traditions offer another way to understand community, identity, and change.

01 / Ocean

The sea as livelihood

For coastal communities, the ocean is a working environment as well as a powerful part of the island’s landscape and imagination.

02 / Land

Volcanic land and local identity

Jeju’s volcanic terrain, coastline, and mountain views create a distinctive setting for everyday life, memory, and tourism.

03 / Haenyeo

Women divers, knowledge, and community.

Haenyeo are women who harvest marine life through diving. Their knowledge, working practices, and community ties are an important part of Jeju’s intangible cultural heritage. UNESCO inscribed the culture of Jeju Haenyeo on its Representative List in 2016.

Memory, identity, continuity

These themes are connected, but they are not interchangeable. Each gives a different perspective on the island.

Memory

How the past is carried

Memorials, public records, testimony, and family stories can shape how later generations encounter a difficult past.

Identity

A distinct island culture

Language, landscape, livelihoods, and shared experiences contribute to a sense of regional identity.

Continuity

Tradition in the present

Living traditions continue to adapt as communities face social, economic, and environmental change.
