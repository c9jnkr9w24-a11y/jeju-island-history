AI use statement

AI can assist a project; people remain responsible for its claims.

The project materials state that LLMs such as ChatGPT and Claude helped draft website code and translate content into Korean. AI was also used for Korean voice-over and automatic captions in CapCut. Team members report manually checking academic translations and facts about the Jeju 4.3 Incident, correcting caption errors, and creating the map coordinates, timeline concept, and overall narrative themselves.
